# amadzai.dev

Personal portfolio site of Amad Zai: who he is, where he has worked, what he has built, and how to reach him.

## Language

**Work**:
The section listing the companies Amad has worked at, newest first. Each row is one role at one company.
_Avoid_: Career, experience, jobs

**Project**:
Something Amad built, shown as a card with a cover, a one-line description, stack pills and links.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, case study

**Cover**:
The Bauhaus-style illustration at the top of a Project card.
_Avoid_: Screenshot, thumbnail

**Recent commits**:
The latest commits Amad pushed to his public GitHub repos.
_Avoid_: Latest commits, activity feed

**Language breakdown**:
The share of each programming language across Amad's public GitHub repos.
_Avoid_: Language stats, tech usage

**Contact Me**:
The modal where visitors find how to reach Amad: email and a cal.com booking link.
_Avoid_: Contact form, get in touch
